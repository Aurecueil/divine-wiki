## What do you want to do?

### Replace existing sounds (1:1 swap)
* **Goal:** Swap an ability SFX, auto-attack, or voice line without changing when or how it triggers.
* **Method:** Open the bank in Quartz, drag and drop new audio (`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, or `.wem`), and save.
* **Guide:** [Easy - Basic Sound Modding](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-easy)

---

### Add more sound variations (editing .bin)
* **Goal:** Expand sound pools (e.g., 10 attack lines instead of 3).
* **Method:** Build new containers in Wwise and load your custom bank first via the champion's `.bin` file.
* **Guide:** [Intermediate - Custom Banks](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-medium)

---

### Add more sounds without editing .bin files
* **Goal:** Expand sound variations or inject audio directly into stock banks without touching champion `.bin` files.
* **Method:** Reconstruct or patch the official `events.bnk` structure directly.
* **Guide:** [Advanced - Reading and Editing events.bnk](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-hard)
* > **Warning:** This is exceptionally difficult. It requires deep hex editing, and proper understanding of banks.

---

### Change sound properties (volume, pitch, attenuation)

* **Recreate the event in Wwise** *(Recommended)*
  * **Pros:** Stable across game patches; full control over mixing curves.
  * **Cons:** Slower initial setup; requires manually matching element IDs.
  * **Guide:** [Intermediate - Custom Banks](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-medium)

* **Patch the official `events.bnk`** *(Quick adjustments)*
  * **Pros:** Fast for one-off tweaks without opening Wwise.
  * **Cons:** Fragile; byte offsets shift whenever Riot updates the bank.
  * **Guide:** [Advanced - Reading and Editing events.bnk](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-hard)

---

### Add custom logic, switches, or announcer packs
* **Goal:** Multi-state music, interactive skin audio, custom buses, or full announcer overhauls.
* **Method:** Author custom hierarchies, switches, and states directly in Wwise.
* **Guide:** [Intermediate - Custom Banks](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-medium)
