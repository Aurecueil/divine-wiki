## What do you want to do?

### Replace existing sounds

- **Goal:** Replace an ability SFX, auto-attack, or voice line.
- **Method:** Edit `audio.bnk` or `audio.wpk` with [Quartz.](https://wiki.divineskins.gg/en/docs/lol/tools/modding-apps/quartz)
- **Guide:** [Easy - Basic Sound Modding](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-easy)

---

### Add extra action/sound to an event

- **Goal:** Add extra sound to play alongside existing sound.
- **Method:** Re-route event in bins to custom proxy event, make proxy event that plays official events + additional action.
- **Guide:** [Intermediate - Custom Banks](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-medium)

---

### Add more sounds or other behaviour alterations 

- **Goal:** Expand sound variations or inject audio directly into stock banks without touching champion `.bin` files.
- **Recreate the event in Wwise** _(recommended)_
  - **Method:** Override existing event with a own event.
  - **Guide:** [Intermediate - Custom Banks](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-medium)
- **Patch the official `events.bnk`** _(quick adjustments)_
  - **Method:** Castle existing elements around or edit their properties.
  - **Guide:** [Advanced - Reading and Editing events.bnk](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-hard)

<Callout type="warning">
  Binary editing in this case can be Very difficult.
</Callout>

---

### Add custom logic, switches, or announcer packs

- **Goal:** Multi-state music, interactive skin audio, custom buses, or full announcer overhauls.
- **Method:** Build custom hierarchies, switches, and states directly in Wwise.
- **Guide:** [Intermediate - Custom Banks](/docs/lol/sounds/sound-medium)
