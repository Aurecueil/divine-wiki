## Tools

* **[Quartz](https://github.com/RitoShark/Quartz)** (Required) — Primary tool for parsing, previewing, and replacing sounds in `.bnk` and `.wpk` archives.
* **[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/download/)** (Recommended) — For trimming audio, matching loudness, and exporting clean waveforms.
* **[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp)** (Optional) — Command-line tool to grab audio samples from online media.

---

## File Formats Overview

League stores audio assets and logic across three core extensions:

* `.bnk`: A Wwise SoundBank. Can hold raw audio data (`audio.bnk`) or hierarchy, bus routing, and event trigger logic (`events.bnk`).
* `.wpk`: A Riot-exclusive format without logic headers. Acts as a flat package strictly containing audio data (commonly used for VO).
* `.wem`: Individual Wwise-encoded compressed audio files packed inside `.bnk` and `.wpk` containers.

> **SFX vs. VO file targets:**  
> * **SFX** (abilities, auto-attacks, impacts, UI) → stored in `audio.bnk`.  
> * **VO** (champion lines, announcer callouts) → stored in `audio.wpk` and governed by `events.bnk`.

## Finding the Required Files

Modifying champion audio requires matching sound archives with the skin's property definition (`.bin`):

| File Role | Format | Example File Path |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Audio Bank / Pack** | `.bnk` or `.wpk` | `ASSETS/Sounds/Wwise2016/VO/en_US/Characters/Yone/Skins/Base/Yone_Base_vo_audio.wpk` |
| **Events Bank** | `.bnk` | `ASSETS/Sounds/Wwise2016/VO/en_US/Characters/Yone/Skins/Base/Yone_Base_vo_events.bnk` |
| **Skin Definition** | `.bin` | `data/characters/Yone/skins/skin0.bin` |

Quartz provides two ways to load these files:
* **From game:** Choose the champion and skin directly from the dropdown to automatically pull assets from your League install.
* **From an existing mod:** Point Quartz to a unpacked mod folder if you are modifying existing community work.

## Step-by-Step: Replacing Sounds

### 1. Open the Sound Bank Tab

Switch to the **Sound Bank** tab in the main Quartz window.

<img src="/wiki-images/quartz-empty-2.webp" alt="Empty Quartz Sound Bank tab" />

### 2. Parse the Game Bank (Left Panel)

1. Select your source files (`.bin`, `audio.bnk`/`audio.wpk`, and `events.bnk`) or choose a game preset.
2. Click **Parse**.
3. The left pane populates with every individual event and sound ID present in the bank. You can click on entries to play audio directly and verify you have the right sound.

<img src="/wiki-images/quartz-2-2.webp" alt="Parsed bank on the left panel" />

### 3. Load Replacement Audio (Right Panel)

Load your custom audio files into the right panel. Quartz natively accepts:
* `.wav` (Uncompressed PCM recommended for best quality)
* `.mp3`
* `.ogg`
* `.wem` (Pre-converted Wwise audio)

> **Automatic conversion:** Quartz automatically converts imported `.wav`, `.mp3`, and `.ogg` files into `.wem` using built-in Wwise encoding. You do not need to convert files manually beforehand.

You can also parse a second official League bank on the right side to borrow sounds from other champions or skins.

<img src="/wiki-images/quartz-3-2.webp" alt="Replacement sounds on the right panel" />

### 4. Replace the Audio

* Drag a sound from the **right panel** directly onto the target sound in the **left panel**.
* The icon and text on the left panel update to indicate a modified entry.
* Click the replaced entry to preview the new sound and confirm volume levels in-engine.

<img src="/wiki-images/quartz4.webp" alt="Dragging a sound to replace it in Quartz" />

### 5. Export the Modified Bank

1. Select the modified bank on the left panel.
2. Click **Save as BNK/WPK**.
3. Save the exported file into your mod workspace, mirroring the exact folder hierarchy League expects.

<img src="/wiki-images/quartz-save-2.webp" alt="Saving the edited bank in Quartz" />

## Packaging the Final Mod

When building your mod package (e.g., for cslol-manager), include only the modified files:

| Mod Target | Required Files to Pack | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SFX Mod** | `..._audio.bnk` | Standalone audio replacements do not require modified event banks or `.bin` files. |
| **VO Mod** | `..._audio.wpk` **AND** `..._events.bnk` | The `events.bnk` holds the string mappings for VO calls. Without saving and including it alongside the `.wpk`, voice triggers will fail. |
